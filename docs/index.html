Frequently asked questions

What are dermal fillers and how do they work?

Dermal fillers are injectable gels that add volume beneath the skin. They smooth lines, plump areas that have lost fullness and refine facial contours. Treatment is quick, and most people return to their normal routine right away.

What are hyaluronic acid fillers?

Hyaluronic acid fillers use a substance your body already produces to attract and hold moisture. They create soft, natural-feeling volume and can be dissolved by your provider if an adjustment is needed.

What can facial filler treatment help with?

Facial filler treatment can restore volume in the cheeks, define the lips, smooth lines around the mouth and balance the chin and jawline. During your consultation we'll review which areas fit your goals.

How long do wrinkle filler injections last?

Longevity depends on the product, the treatment area and your body. Many hyaluronic acid fillers last from several months to a year or more. We'll give you a realistic estimate for your plan.

What are nasolabial fold fillers?

Nasolabial fold fillers soften the lines that run from the sides of the nose to the corners of the mouth. They're among the most common filler treatments and often need only a short appointment.
